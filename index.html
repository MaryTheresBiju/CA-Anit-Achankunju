Regulatory Circulars & Updates

What's New in Taxation & Corporate Law

  • Union Budget Slabs (AY 2025-26): Standard deduction enhanced to ₹75,000 under the New Tax Regime, raising the tax-free rebate threshold to ₹7.75 Lakhs gross.
  • Section 43B(h) MSME Payment Rules: Mandatory payment to micro and small enterprises within 15/45 days to avoid tax deduction disallowances.
  • E-Invoicing Expansion: Mandatory e-invoicing provisions under GST for registered B2B suppliers.
  • Tax Audit u/s 44AB Limits: Turnover threshold of ₹10 Crores applicable where aggregate digital receipts and payments exceed 95%.
